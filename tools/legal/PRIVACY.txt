PRIVACY POLICY — Amharic Captions Pro
Version 1.0

WHAT TRANSCRIPTION NEVER DOES
All speech recognition, correction, speaker labeling and caption placement
happen entirely on your computer. Your audio, video, footage, transcripts,
project files and captions NEVER leave your machine and are never uploaded.
You do not need internet to transcribe.

WHAT THE SOFTWARE DOES SEND
* License activation: when you activate a key, your Machine ID and the key are
  sent to the license server to confirm the key belongs to your machine
  (forged/stolen keys are rejected). A signed, machine-bound lease token is
  returned and verified locally on your machine thereafter.
* Anonymous usage beacon: each time you open the panel, a single request is
  sent with your application version and Machine ID — used to count installs
  and detect abuse. No audio, text, transcript or personal data.
* Free-trial usage: the number of free transcriptions used is synced so the
  trial limit is enforced.

WHAT IS STORED AND WHERE
License data lives on Cloudflare's platform (Workers + D1): Machine ID,
license key, order record (nickname, account used, amount), and the source IP
of activations for fraud detection. Old records are pruned after 30 days. Data
is not sold or shared with any third party other than Cloudflare (hosting) and
Telegram (ordering/support).

LOCAL IDENTITY STORAGE
Your Machine ID is a random identifier stored in your user profile and the
panel's local storage (no personal information). A hashed (one-way) fingerprint
of hostname + username is stored locally so support can recognize records
copied onto another computer; it is never sent anywhere.

DELETION & YOUR RIGHTS
Request deletion or correction of your license/order record any time via
Telegram @sumpak6. Removing your data deactivates the associated key.
