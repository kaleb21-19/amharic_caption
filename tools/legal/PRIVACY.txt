PRIVACY POLICY — Amharic Captions Pro
Version 1.0

WHAT TRANSCRIPTION NEVER DOES
All speech recognition, correction, speaker labeling and caption placement
happen entirely on your computer. Your audio, video, footage, transcripts,
project files and captions NEVER leave your machine and are never uploaded.
You do not need internet to transcribe.

WHAT THE SOFTWARE DOES SEND
* License activation: when you activate a key, your Machine ID and the key are
  sent to the license server to confirm the key belongs to this installation
  (forged/stolen keys are rejected). A signed, installation-bound lease token is
  returned and verified locally on your machine thereafter.
* Usage beacon: when the panel opens, it may send your application version, a
  pseudonymous Machine ID, origin, and network source information to the license
  server. This is used to count installs, detect abuse, and support diagnostics.
  It does not contain audio, text, or transcripts. Telemetry retention is
  bounded separately from license records.
* Free-trial usage: the number of free transcriptions used is synced so the
  trial limit is enforced. Trial counters may be retained as pseudonymous
  abuse-prevention records; request deletion where applicable.

WHAT IS STORED AND WHERE
License data lives on Cloudflare's platform (Workers + D1): Machine ID,
license key, Telegram account identifiers, order record (nickname, account
used, amount), and source IPs for fraud detection. Order history, webhook idempotency records, and short-lived telemetry are
pruned separately; license and trial-abuse records may be retained while
needed to support or protect an active license. Data is not sold. Service providers
include Cloudflare, Vercel (website hosting), and Telegram (ordering/support).

LOCAL IDENTITY STORAGE
Your Machine ID is a random installation identifier stored in your user
profile and the panel's local storage. A hashed fingerprint of your username,
home-directory path, and platform is stored locally to help detect a copied
profile; it is never sent anywhere.

DELETION & YOUR RIGHTS
Request deletion or correction of your license/order record any time via
Telegram @sumpak6. Removing your data deactivates the associated key.
